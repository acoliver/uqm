# Resupply before recovering the Burvixese caster

The northern route starts with 43 flagship crew and five thrusters. The new
earlier route returns to Sol and sells three Furies plus fuel to pay for a
second crew pod, 100 crew, six thrusters and two turning jets. It keeps the
Human cruiser. An Alpha Centauri Melnorme fuel purchase fills the tanks
before the original Burvixese route resumes.

Every purchase uses ordinary communication, shipyard and outfitting inputs.
The flagship has all eleven thrusters before its long hostile transit.
Transit encounters use normal escape controls. The original story assertions,
action deadlines, seed, starting save and overall budgets are unchanged.
No save bytes or provenance were edited.
