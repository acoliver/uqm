# Resupply before the Ilwrath approach

The Burvixese checkpoint starts with 32 flagship crew and five thrusters. Its
long approach crosses hostile patrols before reaching the Ilwrath. Fuel alone
does not provide enough crew or maneuvering for those encounters.

The added route visits Zeeman's Melnorme trader, buys 100 fuel units with the
checkpoint's existing credits, then returns to Sol. It sells one Fury and fuel
to buy a second crew pod, fill the flagship to 100 crew and install three thrusters.
A further Alpha Centauri fuel purchase and Sol resale pay for the remaining
three thrusters and one turning jet. The final fuel purchase spends the
remaining 123 credits before the original Ilwrath leg resumes.

All purchases use the normal communication, shipyard and outfitting inputs.
The route keeps three escorts and leaves Sol with all eleven thrusters. Hostile
transit encounters use the ordinary escape action. The original story and
approach assertions, action deadlines, seed, starting save and overall budgets
are unchanged. No save bytes or provenance were edited.
