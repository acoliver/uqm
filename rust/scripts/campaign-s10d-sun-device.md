# Sun-device fuel plan

This scenario retrieves the Sun Device after the Mycon ambush. The original route bought 6,000 raw fuel, spent 4,725 reaching Beta Brahe, then paid 500 for each of four ordinary encounter retreats. That exhausted the remaining 1,275 before the 194-fuel landing. Dispatch correctly refused to launch the lander.

The existing tank capacity is 6,000, so additional fuel purchases alone cannot repair the route. After the original 6,000-fuel assertion, the scenario uses the outfit menu to sell the first turning jet, install a standard fuel tank in empty module slot 0, and purchase another 1,000 fuel. These transactions leave the original gun in slot 15 and every existing assertion unchanged.

The saved 737 RU cover the purchases: the first 800 fuel costs 160 RU, selling the jet returns 500 RU, the tank costs 500 RU, and the additional fuel costs 200 RU. The remaining balance is 377 RU. The extra tank increases capacity to 11,000; the scenario verifies the actual tank installation and 7,000 fuel before departure.

With the original four retreats, 7,000 minus 4,725 minus 2,000 leaves 275, enough for the 194-fuel landing. Fuel consumption, retreat costs, combat rules, landing eligibility and the scenario's global budgets are unchanged. Every original step remains in its original order.
